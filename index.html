    What is Datewise?

    Datewise is a free daily puzzle. You get five events from history, science, sport, film and India. Put them in order from oldest to newest. Everyone gets the same puzzle each day.

    How is it scored?

    You have four tries. After each try, cards in the right spot turn green and lock. The others show an arrow pointing to where they belong. Solve it in fewer tries to keep your streak.

    How to play

    1. Order the five events from oldest at the top to newest at the bottom.
    2. Swap cards by dragging one onto another, or tap two cards.
    3. Lock in to check. You get four tries.
    ✓ Right spot. The card locks.
    ↑ Belongs higher (older).
    ↓ Belongs lower (newer).
